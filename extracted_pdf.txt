Abstract 
The Smart Connects: AI-Powered platform for Connecting Individuals to 
Communities project seeks to tackle the difficulty individuals face when looking for and 
getting involved in communities which suit their needs (social, professional, academic, and 
personal) interests, and objectives. With so much information distributed amongst varying 
sources, from social networking profiles and groups to event pages, independent websites and 
much more, the discovery process can be difficult and very time consuming. To ease this 
challenge, it is proposed the development of an intelligent platform to connect willing 
individuals to active communities.  
It enables users to register their interests, view communities and view events, whereas 
community leaders can set up a profile and advertise activities to the community to garner the 
interest of prospective members. Recommendation functions will be able to suggest 
communities to the user based on his or her interests. The goal of this system is to have a 
singular platform for communities to gather information on, and make it easy for members to 
participate in communities and build network relations 
Keywords: Community Platform, AI Recommendation System, Community Engagement, 
Social Networking Platform, Event Discovery System

Chapter 1: INTRODUCTION 
1.1 
Introduction  
Communities play a pivotal role in fostering knowledge sharing, cooperation and 
interaction in today's digital world. There are many different kinds of communities like social 
clubs, professional groups, interest-based groups, voluntary associations etc. that provide us 
with ways to interact with others who have similar interests or objectives. Networking with 
others who have similar interests, personal and social development and social interactions 
can be provided by these communities and may be appealing to those who seek connections. 
Although the number of both offline and online communities is increasing, finding 
appropriate ones based on the desired topic and interests remains difficult for many. This is 
particularly true of students and professionals who have recently moved to other 
cities/countries to attend school or start new jobs. These communities generally advertise 
information through social media profiles, messaging applications, or individual Web pages 
making it difficult to efficiently and easily locate appropriate ones (Shu-Hsien Liao, 2021). 
Existing platforms such as Facebook Groups allow communities to create and manage 
groups within them. However users have to manually find groups that fit their interests; this 
becomes very time consuming to find the correct communities, they do not always provide 
intelligently recommends communities based on users behavior and interests and 
consequently users are not able to easily find the appropriate communities; Many 
communities on the other hand cannot find more and more participants interested to be a part 
of them (Nil-Jana Akpinar, 2025).

12 
 
In order to solve the previously presented problems, Smart Connects: AI-Powered 
Platform for Connecting Individuals to Communities was introduced as an all-in-one 
platform, where individuals could easily find their communities and events that correspond to 
their interests, thus connecting people that search for activities with communities that search 
for members with smart recommendation features and a structured informative platform. 
1.2 
Problem Background  
In the present digital era, communities and social groups play a significant role in 
providing a platform where individuals could communicate, share information and participate 
in social and professional activities. There are numerous communities such as clubs, 
volunteer organizations, student groups, and groups that meet to talk about hobbies, that 
enables individuals to build a relationship, share their knowledge, enhance their professional 
or personal skills (Paolo Spagnoletti, 2022) . Despite the plethora of communities that exist 
today, most people find it extremely challenging to identify communities that suit their 
interest, requirements and their location. 
The main reason for this problem lies in the distribution of information about communities 
over a wide range of platforms like social media page, messengers, websites, event pages, 
and hence an individual needs to go through all these information manually to discover new 
communities and events which are of interest to them (Ellison et al., 2021). Such effort could 
be very tedious and may cause individuals, especially those new to a new city or country, to 
miss out on new and relevant communities/events. 


--- PAGE 13 ---
13 
 
In present, we have communities platform like Facebook Groups, other community 
platform to organize and publish their information. These platform rely on manual searching, 
category browsing and often does not provide results according to individual interest. Mostly 
users will find a number of unrelated groups, it does not necessarily means that this 
community fits with individual needs. While relatively smaller or new community will face 
problems in being found (Gediminas Adomavicius, 2021). 
Also, in the absence of smart recommendation in many existing platform makes the 
process of finding a suitable community less efficient. Since it does not intelligently provide 
the communities according to user interest, it does not help individuals to find related 
communities while at the same time unable to provide sufficient exposure to communities to 
get new members. In this condition, there is a need of one place and intelligent platform 
which can gather the information about the communities and provides exposure for 
communities, at the same time helps people to find communities fitting with their needs. 
1.3 
Project Aim  
The objective of this project is to build a smart connects platform an AI powered platform 
which aims at finding and connecting individuals with communities according to their 
interests. It aims at easing out the task of finding communities and events.  
1.4 
Objectives  
• To create an centralized platform, where users can explore and connect with different 
communities and groups. 


--- PAGE 14 ---
14 
 
• To allow users to register and specify their interests in order to get relevant 
community suggestions. 
• To let community organizers to create profiles and upload information about their 
communities and upcoming events. 
• To improve community visibility and participation by providing an easy and user-
friendly platform for engagement. 
1.5 
Scope  
1.5.1 Tasks to be Executed 
• User Registration and Profile Setup: User registration, profiling and declaration of 
interests allowing personalized community recommendations.  
• Community Management: Community organizer registration and profile creation with 
detailed community information (activities, goals, contacts). 
• Community and Event Listing: Provide a structured platform where communities can 
post information about their groups and upcoming events for users to explore.  
• Search and Recommendation Features: Platform to showcase the groups and their 
events to the user.  
• User Interface Design: User search functionality and some AI-based recommendations 
to find the right communities.  
• User Feedback and Evaluation: User feedback collection mechanism (surveys, usability 
testing) to determine the effectiveness and ease of use.

1.5.2 Constraints 
• Technology Limitations: The recommended services will only be primitive because 
there wasn't enough time and technology for developing the features in a full length of 
time during development process.  
• Availability Data: The platform will not contain big external database but will use 
manually registered community at the beginning.  
• Development Resources: The project development will be made under a certain amount 
of time which is available during the final year's academic project.  
1.5.3 What Will Be Done 
• Design and build a platform linking users to communities according to their interests. 
• Include user registration, community profiles and community browsing function. 
• Include event listings and community details for higher user interaction. 
• Perform basic user testing for system and usability evaluation.  
1.5.4 What Will Not Be Done 
• No full integration will be done on this stage with external social media applications or 
any instant messenger. 
• The sophisticated machine learning models to predict complex behavior will not be 
developed in this first version. 
• Development of the mobile application will not be part of the project, web-based system 
will be the focus.

1.7 
Target User 
1.7.1  Individuals New to a City or Country 
Students or professionals that are newly immigrated or moved to a new city/country 
struggle to know which groups or social circles they are part of. With the application, 
students or professionals can find the communities and events that best fit their needs, 
facilitate integration into the new social structure.  
1.7.2 Students 
Students will look for academic groups or hobby/volunteering groups or social 
circles. With the application, students will be able to easily find student clubs, study circles or 
social circles related to students' needs.  
1.7.3 Professionals and Job Seekers 
Professionals looking to broaden their network or gain new skills can leverage this platform 
to find professional communities, industry groups and networking events relevant to their 
professional lives.  
1.7.4 Community Organizers 
Community leaders, clubs, NGOs and social groups can utilize this platform to set up a page 
profile for their group/club, publicize events and attract new members.   
1.7.5 Older Adults and Elderly People 
Socially isolated older adults can connect with nearby groups, social clubs, or other activities 
through this platform that can help them develop relationships and participate in society. The

1.7.6 Individuals Seeking Social Engagement

special interest, or community activities can use this platform to discover these opportunities

Users are able to discover and find communities in relation to category, i.e. 
Activities/hobbies, volunteering, professional and personal development, society or social 
networks etc. Information on the communities are made available in an orderly way.  
1.8.3 Community Profiles and Information Pages 
Each community should have a specific page where we present data like community 
description, activities, social media page, contact persons and some testimonials from users.

1.9 
Alignment with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) 
SDG 11 Sustainable Cities and Communities aims to build inclusive safe and well-
connected communities. The Smart Connects system addresses this SDG by giving people an 
easy-to-access place to find and interact with communities and events in their area which 
match their interests. This enhances the access to information available to people while 
simultaneously minimizing the barrier between individuals and community events. 
The Smart Connects system will enhance the ability of users to be actively involved as 
the easy way to find communities and stay up to date about upcoming events makes users 
more aware and therefore increases community involvement. It acts to bring together 
individuals and improve social interaction between them. By also making it easier for 
community organizers to get the word out it also helps communities be more engaged. 
In summary the Smart Connects system facilitates inclusion and the building of 
community through a streamlined and straightforward digital application. It will facilitate the 
building of integrated social environments and the active social engagement of users while 
strengthening connections. 
1.10 Overview of the IR 
This project proposes Smart Connects, an AI-powered system aimed at matching users 
to communities based on their interests, purposes, and social requirements. It aims at 
eliminating confusion caused by various disjointed information channels regarding 
communities and events and providing a one-stop shop and structured database for easily 
locating communities or events. This allows users to conveniently locate relevant 


--- PAGE 21 ---
21 
 
communities while at the same time providing opportunities for community organizers to 
post events and activities to their interested attendees. 
This research investigates current systems and previous studies to identify deficiencies 
such as lack of personalization, inaccessible resources and scattered information; explores 
technical aspects of the system including tools, technology and system design; employs a 
structured development process supported by surveys to elicit user needs, pain points, 
interests, and preferences; identifies features like the ability to search for communities, view 
events, and get personalized community recommendations. 
The interface of the system is planned to be user friendly and very simple to make it 
easy for various people regardless of their level of technical expertise to use it. In general the 
project would aim to create an efficient community discovering platform that will increase 
user engagement and facilitate better connections through a structured system.

2.1.1 Community Recommendation Systems in Online Social Networks 
In this study the recommendation systems for finding relevant communities or groups 
within the online social network are investigated. It is describe how a recommendation 
system can find suitable communities for user by processing user's behavior, interests and 
relationships. Machine learning, data mining technology are utilized to process huge amount 
of data of users to find relationship or similarity between the users' interest. Collaborative 
filtering and content-based filtering were also discussed that these methods are commonly 
used for making recommendation system to finding communities matching to user profile 
(Kaur, 2020). 
The primary goal of the study was to find a better approach to discover relevant 
communities from the online social networks that having tens of thousands communities or 
groups. Manual browsing through all of communities is inefficient and can be time 
consuming in large scale social network. So recommendation system is used to automatically 
recommend some relevant communities to users by considering users' profile. As the results 
shown, recommendation system with individual preference, historical activity on social 
network would highly increase the user engagement, and users can find a target community 
easily (Kaur, 2020).

2.3.5 System Architecture of Smart Connects 
The Smart Connects system has a system architecture based on modern web 
application architecture where frontend, backend, database and server are separated. The 
separated layers enable the system to scale, be maintained efficiently and allow easy 
communication among the layers of the system. The platform is a client-server architecture 
that users interact with a frontend interface that communicates with backend services to 
handle their requests, retrieve data from the database. 
The frontend layer in the Smart Connects system is developed with Next.js that 
produces a responsive and interactive interface to interact with the users. This layer allows 
the users to sign up account, search communities, see event lists, browse community page 
and other tasks. Next.js manages the routing between pages, renders the components and 
connects with the backend with API calls. Next.js also increases the performance and loads 
pages faster.  
The backend is developed using Node.js with the framework Express.js. The backend 
layer is the "backbone of the application", responsible for handling all the logic and requests 
received from the frontend. Its main concerns include user verification, handling community 
searches, event managing and data validation. The Express.js framework provides facilities 
for routing the requests to different endpoints and for developing mid-ware that are useful to 
help the application work smoothly. The communication between the frontend and the 
database can be handled by the backend efficiently and a lot clearer with the help of 
Express.js. 


--- PAGE 49 ---
49 
 
The database layer is maintained using PostgreSQL. It stores all the data for the 
application, such as user accounts, information about communities, the events on the 
platform, categories and keywords used for search. PostgreSQL ensures that all data is 
reliably stored and allows powerful queries needed to search effectively for the information 
about communities the user desires on the platform.  
The server layer takes advantage of Nginx as a web server and reverse proxy in order 
to manage the traffic being sent to our application and render it effectively. System 
performance is enhanced as Nginx can control the amount of clients requesting the system 
and assist in load balancing as well as the communication to the client to ensure security. 
All in all the Smart Connects system architecture enables all components to be well 
integrated. Next.js will serve as a development framework for the front-end, Node.js and 
Express.js as back-end handling, and PostgreSQL as our database and the Nginx web server 
in order to cater the application on the server side providing a robust and efficient 
architecture that links users with various communities and events.

Chapter 3: METHODOLOGY 
Methodology for development is the way in which the system can be planned, 
designed, developed and implemented. The development methodology ensures the project

the Smart Connects project, considering the number of elements to be developed, from the

3.1 
System Development Methodology 
3.1.1 Introduction 
Agile development with iterations is the process in which the Smart Connects 
platform is being developed. The agile development methodology creates software in small 
pieces of working functionality which are often referred to as iterations. Each iteration 
comprises requirement, analysis, design, development, test and review and the outcome is a 
working piece of the application that is then tested and refined in next iterations.This 
approach is appropriate for web based platform where functionality and user experience can 
change over time. In contrast to building the complete application, the Agile model permits 
the application designers to introduce individual features like user registration, community 
searching, events managing and recommendation engine at each iteration cycle and get 
feedback from users and supervisors (Samar Al-Saqqa, 2020). 
3.1.2 Methodology Choice and Justification 
The Smart Connects project uses the Agile development methodology as it fits well 
with features such as adaptability, improvement and incremental development. The platform 


--- PAGE 52 ---
52 
 
has a range of aspects such as frontend implementation, backend implementation (APIs, 
databases) and the recommendation features to take into account. With these developments it 
becomes possible to adjust the development of these systems to any changes/ improvements 
made within the development of Smart Connects by using shorter development cycles. 
A benefit to using Agile development is the ability to react to any changes within the 
requirements. Within the development of Smart Connects the recommendations made by 
users/supervisors to add/remove specific elements such as; search filters, community 
features/recommendations, and User interface improvements can be easily adapted. Without 
having to impact the entire system structure there can be changes implemented for an 
improvement within the system. 
An important factor to the Agile methodology is reducing risk factors within 
development. Developing the system in an incremental fashion would allow the use of core 
elements such as user authentication, community listing and database functions to be tested at 
the beginning stages of development. If any problems occur it will only impact small portions 
of the overall system making for easier fixability and making it more stable.

instance, if a user experience error in the event display, login or search feature

performance can be improved and a system that is functioning smoothly and is 
good for the user can be developed. 
2. Adaptive Maintenance

Adaptive maintenance helps the system function normally as the system itself is

is done when the system may have to be updated due to new frameworks, new

3. Perfective Maintenance 
Perfective maintenance involves enhancing the performance of the system and to

4. Preventive Maintenance 
Preventive maintenance aims at preventing any future errors or problems from the 
system and maintain a good system health by updating or improving the code and 
to check for security errors on the system so as to eliminate system errors.

3.3 
System Architecture 
The system architecture of Smart Connects represents the overall working flow of the 
platform from user interaction to data processing and output. The system follows a web-
based architecture where users interact through the frontend interface, and all requests are 
processed through the backend and database. 
The process begins when a user accesses the platform through a web browser using a 
mobile or desktop device. The frontend, built using Next.js, provides the user interface where 
users can register, log in, search for communities, and view events. User actions such as 
searching or selecting a community are sent as requests to the backend server. 
The backend, developed using Node.js and Express.js, processes these requests and 
handles the application logic. It retrieves relevant data from the PostgreSQL database, which 
stores user information, community details, and event data. The backend ensures that the 
correct data is fetched and sent back to the frontend. 
The database acts as the central storage system, maintaining structured records of users, 
communities, and events. It supports efficient data retrieval and ensures that the system 
remains organized and scalable. 
Finally, the processed data is displayed to the user through the frontend interface in a 
clear and structured format. This architecture ensures smooth communication between 
components and provides a reliable and user-friendly platform for discovering communities 
and events.
