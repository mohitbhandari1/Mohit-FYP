The Smart Connects platform provides two types of users: general users and community organizers. General users can browse communities, join groups, and RSVP to events. Users who wish to create their own community or represent an organization can apply through the "Start a New Group" feature.

When applying, the applicant is required to provide details such as the organization or community name, address, contact information, target audience, age group, category of the community, official documents, and other supporting information. Once the application is submitted, it is forwarded to the system administrator for review and verification.

The administrator examines the submitted information and verifies the legitimacy of the organization or individual. If the application satisfies all requirements and the provided documents are valid, the administrator approves the application. Upon approval, the system generates access credentials using the username provided during registration and issues a temporary password.

After receiving approval, the community organizer can log in to the platform, change the password, manage their community profile, and create events. The organizer can also update community information, publish announcements, and interact with community members through the platform.

Application Workflow
User clicks "Start a New Group".
User completes and submits the application form.
Application is sent to the System Administrator.
Administrator reviews the organization details and uploaded documents.
If approved, the system creates the community account and activates the community page.
The organizer receives login credentials and can update the password after first login.
The organizer can then create events, manage members, and publish community updates.
